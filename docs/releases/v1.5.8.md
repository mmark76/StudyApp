# StudyApp v1.5.8 release scope

Source: reviewed PR #348 on main, following the published v1.5.7 release.
This compatible patch completes the approved Workspace BETA presentation polish.

## Changes

- Present Summary and Progress in the central Workspace as concise results or
  empty states, with a discreet action that opens their existing full workflows
  in a modal.
- Keep the Summary creation and Progress backup, restore and archive controls
  out of the central results area while preserving their established workflows.
- Give the unavailable AI-Assisted Structuring control a soft warning surface
  with a clear high-contrast unavailable label.
- Keep right-column tool-card titles emphasized while tool descriptions use the
  regular shared Workspace BETA type weight.
- Correct the Workspace handoff so the Summary and Progress actions always open
  the complete setup modal rather than retaining the result-only view.

## Compatibility and boundaries

- IndexedDB schemas, existing study content, backup/restore and archive formats
  are unchanged.
- No automatic AI tool generation, remote processing, RAG activation, content
  upload or AI permission change is introduced. RAG remains under construction
  and unavailable.
- Local ChatGPT direct conversation, selected-model behavior, streaming,
  `store: false`, consent boundaries and Local packaging remain unchanged.
- The Web/PWA edition continues not to create an in-app chat.
- Automated Local tests use mocked loopback responses. A real signed-in Windows
  Local conversation still requires the documented manual release check.
